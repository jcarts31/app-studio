> "It let me pick the same upgrade more than once which didn't do anything to have two."
→ Fixed in v2: owned upgrades are no longer offered. (Design question for later: should some upgrades stack on purpose?)

→ Fixed in v2: floor 1 deals neighbouring sizes only (e.g. d6/d8); spread widens by floor (1, 2, 3 steps; 5 in endless).

> "Even after a few basic upgrades I could see real choices popping up faster and making way more sense."
→ Spike signal: the core pick works once a build exists. The weak spot is the opening rounds.
