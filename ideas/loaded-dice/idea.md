# Loaded Dice (working title) — idea

Dice-betting roguelite for Android. Each round the player sees 2+ dice of different sizes (d4–d20+) and bets on which will roll highest. Run upgrades (e.g. +1 to a chosen die, triple a d4, advantage on d6) and one-time consumables (reroll, heal, change a die's size) bend the result. Wrong bet = lose HP equal to the gap. Later floors add more dice and sizes; builds like a "d4 build". Vibe: Balatro + D&D + casino.

Owner answers (2026-11-06): unlocks carry over between runs; fixed floors with bosses, endless after; money = decide later.

## Growth ideas (2026-11-06, owner + director, not yet scoped)
- Opponent dice scale by floor with their own effects (cursed dice, +2 red, roll twice).
- Bosses break a rule (hide a die's size, lock a colour, force second-best pick), not just hit harder.
- Margin of victory earns in-run gold/souls for a shop between floors (earned only, never bought).
- More than one decision per round: spend to reroll the table, peek a die before picking.
- Meta unlocks: new colours, new dice (d100, coin), starting upgrades, difficulty tiers.
- **Criticals (owner):** a die's top face = crit, a 1 = fumble, every size; opponents too. Enables crit-range / crit-power upgrades; small dice crit more often.
- **Classes (owner):** pick a class at start (e.g. Paladin, Wizard, Rogue, Fighter), each with a starting kit, one signature rule, and its own skill pool unlocked by levelling up during a run. Launch with 2-3, add more later. Generic fantasy names only; nothing branded D&D.
- Scope note: with classes the MVP is likely L (5-7 milestones).
