# Sparrow: design direction

**Look:** Morning Pages. **Locked as v1:** 2026-11-09 (owner).

## Feel
A quiet desk at first light. Soft sage and mist, a book-like serif for everything the owner
writes, and plenty of room to think. The journal feels private and unhurried; the posting
tools stay out of the way until they're wanted.

## Feels like / doesn't feel like
| Feels like | Not like |
|---|---|
| A leather journal by a window | A social media dashboard |
| Early-morning quiet time | A busy church bulletin |
| A trusted friend's handwriting | Inspirational poster clichés |

## Principles
- **Journal first.** Writing gets the widest column and the serif; tools sit beside it.
- **Calm by default.** No red badges, no counters racing upward, no urgency colors except the
  amber "needs a decision" state.
- **The owner decides.** Every post, reply and new asset shows an explicit approve step.
- **Private stays private.** Each entry has an "Okay to share from this entry" switch.

## Owner decisions
- 2026-11-09: direction A, Morning Pages (over Stained Glass and Riverbank Studio).
- 2026-11-09: name Sparrow (Matthew 10:29–31).
- 2026-11-09: key screens locked as v1 ("I'll need to use it for a bit before I decide on
  changes"). Changes after real use come back through design first.
