# Sparrow: motion, sound and feel

## Motion
- Screens and panels settle in softly (about 220 ms, gentle ease-out). No bouncing.
- Approving a post: the row warms briefly to the tint color (about 400 ms), then shows Ready.
- One orchestrated moment per action at most; no idle animation.
- Respect the system's reduced-motion setting: swap motion for instant changes.

## Sound (placeholders until the asset pass)
| Moment | Sound | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Post or week approved | a single small bell, warm and short | the only "reward" sound |
| Reply sent | a soft paper rustle | |
| Reviewer flag appears | none | flags should never alarm |
| Hold all posts | a low, quiet tone | confirms the pause |

Sounds are off by default for anything repeated and can be turned off entirely in settings.

## Never
No streaks, no follower-count celebrations, no red badges, no confetti, no "you're on fire".
Numbers in Insights inform; they never reward.
