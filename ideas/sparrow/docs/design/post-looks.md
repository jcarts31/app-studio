# Sparrow: post looks (v1, 2026-11-11)

Each kind of post has its own look, all in the Morning Pages family. Mockups:
https://claude.ai/artifact/FntS4RXFMYuCfiqwsBG7dG (second row, "A look for each kind of post").
Card sizes: square 1080×1080 and portrait 1080×1350. Backgrounds come from approved library
images and art.

| Kind | Without a fitting image | With a fitting image |
|---|---|---|
| Verse | Plain sage card: verse in Literata, gold rule, reference and translation | Verse on a dark ink panel at the bottom of the photo |
| Quote (trusted voice) | Framed: deep sage, thin gold frame, italic quote, name and work | Full darkened photo, quote centered in white, gold name line |
| Your words | Journal page: light page, gold margin line, "From my journal" | Photo on top, your words on a sage band below |
| Prayer | Quiet page: sage tint, centered prayer, "Amen." in sage, gold lines above and below | (same; option A chosen) |

Owner decisions:
- 2026-11-11: a distinct look per kind; favorites 1 (sage verse) and 3 (verse over photo).
- 2026-11-11: quotes use both looks; prayers get their own look, option A (quiet page).
- 2026-11-11 (default): your words use both looks.
- These are v1. The owner expects to revisit colors, image types and music once their brand
  identity settles; that becomes a brand pass with app-design, likely alongside the asset pass
  once the library has real content. Keep looks in `content/cards.yaml` so changing them needs
  no code.
