# Sparrow: key screens (v1)

Mockups: https://claude.ai/artifact/XUdxx59t1HC4tDQuyQwvU4 (desktop window, 1280×800, fluid).
All screens share the left navigation: logo, Journal, This week (count to review), Library,
Inbox (new count), Insights; at the bottom, the next scheduled post and **Hold all posts**.

## 1. Welcome (first launch)
- **Purpose:** introduce Sparrow and get the first entry written.
- **Layout:** sage brand panel (logo, name, one-line promise, Matthew 10:29 KJV); setup list of
  four steps: write your first entry, teach Sparrow your voice, set your guardrails (on by
  default: nothing political, no controversial figures, check the news first, only verified
  quotes), connect your accounts (official sign-in; no passwords seen).
- **Interactions:** "Start your first entry" opens the Journal; steps 2–4 can be done any time.

## 2. Journal (home)
- **Purpose:** write and revisit entries; see what Sparrow is tracking.
- **Layout:** entry list (search; date, title, verse or influence) with a "Sparrow noticed"
  note (recurring verses, answered prayers); entry (date, title, tags, verse block with
  translation, reflection, prayer with "Mark answered"); draft panel (Okay to share switch,
  post preview, asset used, reviewer result, Add to this week, Rewrite it).
- **Interactions:** autosave while typing; verse references become chips; switching off "Okay
  to share" removes the draft panel's actions.

## 3. This week (core loop)
- **Purpose:** review and approve the coming week in one sitting.
- **Layout:** header with ready/decision counts and "Approve the N ready posts"; one row per
  scheduled post: day and time, asset thumbnail, first line, source and platforms, status
  (Ready / Needs a decision). Flagged rows expand an amber reviewer note with two clear choices.
- **Interactions:** approving plays the soft chime; open a row to edit text, asset, time or
  platforms.

## 4. Library
- **Purpose:** the approved images, music and clips Sparrow may use, plus new finds to approve.
- **Layout:** filters (All, Images, Music, Clips, Waiting for you); "new finds" strip with
  Approve / Skip (Play for audio); image grid with source and use count; music rows with
  length, platform clearance and use count.
- **Interactions:** add own files; retire an asset so it isn't reused.

## 5. Inbox and insights
- **Purpose:** answer replies in one place; see what's resonating.
- **Layout:** reply cards (who, platform, post, time, comment) with a drafted reply (Send reply,
  Edit, Just like it); heavy or personal comments get no draft, only "Write a reply" and "Save
  to my journal". Insights panel: kinds of posts vs comments, Sparrow's takeaway, audience
  summary, followers per platform.
- **Interactions:** nothing is sent without a tap.
