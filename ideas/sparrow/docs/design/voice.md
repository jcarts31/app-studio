# Sparrow: voice and tone

Two voices live in Sparrow. Keep them apart.

## 1. The app's voice (buttons, notes, empty states, errors)
Quiet, warm, plain. Like a thoughtful friend who helps you get ready in the morning.

- Short sentences, everyday words, sentence case.
- Says exactly what will happen ("Approve the 5 ready posts", "Send reply").
- Never rushes or nags: no "Don't miss out", no streaks, no guilt about days skipped.
- Doesn't preach. The app supports the owner's faith; it doesn't add sermons of its own.
- Errors say what happened and what to do, without apologizing or blaming.

**Use:** entry, prayer, verse, post, reply, this week, approve, hold, okay to share, answered.
**Avoid:** content, engagement, algorithm, optimize, crush it, viral, blessed (as filler), AI magic.

### Example lines
- Primary button: "Add to this week"
- Weekly approve: "Approve the 5 ready posts"
- Hold control: "Hold all posts"
- Empty journal: "Nothing here yet. Start with a verse that's been on your mind."
- Empty inbox: "No new replies. Enjoy the quiet."
- Reviewer flag: "I couldn't confirm who said this. Post it without a name, or swap in a quote with a known source?"
- Heavy comment: "This one sounds personal and heavy. It's better in your own words."
- Sign-in expired: "Instagram signed you out. Sign in again to keep posting there."
- Posted: "Posted to Facebook, Instagram and Threads."
- Approved: "Approved. It goes out Thursday at 8:00 am."

## 2. The owner's voice (drafted posts and replies)
Sparrow writes posts and replies *as the owner*, learned from the journal, past posts, edits
and approvals. Until the voice profile exists, defaults:

- First person, honest, specific: real moments (Grandma's kitchen, Thursday's interview).
- The verse or quote carries the weight; the owner's words frame why it mattered.
- Warm, never preachy or performative. No guilt hooks, no "type Amen", no promised blessings
  for sharing.
- A few hashtags at most, only where the platform expects them.
- Never invent experiences, people or feelings the owner didn't write.
